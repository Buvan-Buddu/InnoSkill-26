1 Problem Analyst — Global Inclusive Hiring Breakdown
  • Systemic Talent Exclusion: 1.3 Billion people globally live with disabilities, facing a catastrophic 70-80% unemployment rate in developing nations and 50-70% in developed nations.
  • The "Accommodation Wall": Standard recruitment pipelines force candidates into rigid, visual-only, text-dense, and mouse-dependent flows that fail assistive technologies.
  • Pre-Interview Algorithmic Drop-off: Legacy ATS software automatically discards candidates with non-linear career histories or accommodation requirements.
  • Interview Assessment Discrimination: AI video hiring tools penalize non-standard speech, stuttering, neurodivergent eye-contact, or motor tremors as "low confidence."
  • Information Asymmetry: Candidates fear disclosing accommodation needs; employers lack structured workflows to declare or verify workplace accessibility readiness.
2 Root Cause Analyst — Why Legacy Architectures Fail
🏗️ Single-Modality Architecture Root Cause 1
  • Legacy HR systems (Workday, Greenhouse, Taleo) built on 2005 desktop-first assumptions (screen + mouse + keyboard).
  • Accessibility is treated as a cosmetic overlay plugin rather than foundational DOM tokens.
⚖️ Fear of Disclosure & Legal Ambiguity Root Cause 2
  • 61% of candidates conceal disabilities fearing unconscious hiring bias and immediate resume filtering.
  • Recruiters fear discussing accommodations due to compliance litigation ambiguities under ADA Title I.
🤖 AI Machine Learning Training Bias Root Cause 3
  • ATS scoring algorithms trained on historical corporate resumes systematically downrank non-linear resumes.
  • Standard Speech-to-Text models fail on dysarthric speech, accents, stuttering, and non-verbal modes.
🏢 Post-Hiring Support Vacuum Root Cause 4
  • Hiring platforms terminate workflow at offer acceptance, abandoning candidate during onboarding.
  • No verified feedback loop exists for candidate communities to rate employer accessibility promises.
