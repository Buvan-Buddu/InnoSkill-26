## 1. 🎯 Problem Analyst — Global Inclusive Hiring Breakdown

* **Systemic Talent Exclusion:** 1.3 Billion people live with disabilities globally, facing 70–80% unemployment in developing nations and 50–70% in developed markets.
* **The "Accommodation Wall":** Standard recruitment funnels enforce visual-only, mouse-dependent, text-heavy steps that break assistive screen readers.
* **Pre-Interview Algorithmic Filtering:** Outdated ATS keyword parsers auto-reject non-linear career paths and non-traditional timelines.
* **Interview Assessment Discrimination:** Automated video interviewing tools penalize non-standard eye contact, dysarthric speech, and motor tremors as "low confidence."
* **Information Asymmetry:** 61% of candidates hide accommodation needs out of fear; employers lack structured frameworks to evaluate or verify workplace accessibility readiness.

## 8. 📋 Complete Feature List & Architecture Breakdown

### Layer 1: Multimodal Adaptive Interface
* **Web Speech API Engine:** Real-time speech recognition and synthesis with custom voice command registry.
* **NATO Phonetic Alphabet Spelling Mode:** Enables precise alphanumeric form entry for users with speech variations or visual impairments.
* **MediaPipe ASL Vision System:** Camera-based hand landmark tracking recognizing core sign language navigation commands.
* **Dynamic Accessibility Styler:** Real-time DOM contrast, typography, and font sizing overrides without page reloads.

### Layer 2: Resume Intelligence & Upskilling
* **Semantic OCR Document Parser:** Extracts structured career data from PDF and DOCX files.
* **ATS Compatibility Scoring:** Evaluates resume formatting, keyword density, and structural clarity.
* **AI Skill Gap Analyzer:** Maps candidate capabilities against market demand and generates step-by-step learning roadmaps.
* **Conversational Career Assistant:** 24/7 AI mentor providing career guidance, resume suggestions, and application tips.

### Layer 3: 5-Dimension Explainable Matching Engine
$$\text{Compatibility Score} = 0.35(S) + 0.20(E) + 0.15(G) + 0.15(W) + 0.15(A)$$
* **Verification Confidence Badges:** Source tagging (*Verified, Employer-Reported, Candidate-Reported, Inferred*).
* **Physical & Commute Index:** Evaluates wheelchair entrances, accessible restrooms, elevators, and parking.
* **Responsible AI Bias Audit Layer:** Ensures accommodation requirements never negatively penalize candidate ranking.

### Layer 4: Adaptive Mock Interview Suite
* **STAR-Method Behavioral Analysis:** Evaluates Situation, Task, Action, and Result structured responses.
* **Acoustic Speech Tolerance:** Normalizes for stuttering, dysarthria, and natural pauses.
* **Live Visual Feedback Radar:** Scores technical clarity, structure, and communication without gaze penalties.

### Layer 5: Employer Portal & Post-Hiring Hub
* **Accessibility Readiness Scorecard:** Comprehensive enterprise audit of workplace physical and digital infrastructure.
* **Enterprise ATS Connectors:** Out-of-the-box integration hooks for SAP SuccessFactors, Workday, and Greenhouse.
* **Workplace Accommodation Generator:** Generates 1-click equipment and software accommodation requests for HR.
* **Candidate Community Feedback Loop:** Enables verified candidates to rate and audit employer accessibility claims.
